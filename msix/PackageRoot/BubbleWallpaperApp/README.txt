Bubble Wallpaper App

Double-click the "Open Bubble Wallpaper App.cmd" file in the parent folder to launch this as its own app-style window.

Controls:
- Click or tap bubbles to pop them.
- Use Fullscreen for a full wallpaper-like screen.
- Use Save PNG to export the current frame as an image.
- Sound can be toggled on or off.
- The app icon lives at assets/bubble-wallpaper-icon.png.
